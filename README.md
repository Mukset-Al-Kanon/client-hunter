# ⚡ Client Hunter OS · Command Center

An automated Lead Generation, AI Auditing, and Outreach Command Center built for selling **High-Converting Websites** and **Speed-to-Lead Automations** to high-ticket local businesses in the USA, UK, Canada, and Australia.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
Crafted to follow a **Soft Neumorphic Luxury & Pillowy Depth** aesthetic:
- **Canvas:** Soft warm off-white (`#EFF1F5`)
- **Accents:** Terracotta / Burnt Orange-Red (`#EB5738`)
- **Contrast:** Deep Obsidian (`#111216`)
- **Cards:** Crisp white with 28px pillowy rounded corners and diffuse depth shadows.
- **Typography:** Modern geometric Plus Jakarta Sans.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open PowerShell or Terminal in this folder:
   ```bash
   python server.py
   ```
2. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:7000
   ```

---

## 🛠️ Features Included

1. **Lead Scraping & Enrichment:**
   - Filter by Niche (`Roofing`, `Solar`, `HVAC`, `Plumbing`, `MedSpa`, `Dental`, etc.)
   - Filter by City / State (`Dallas, TX`, `Miami, FL`, `Austin, TX`, `London, UK`)
   - Auto-detection of **No Website** (Hot Web Offer 🔥) vs **Has Website** (Automation & Speed-to-Lead Offer).

2. **⚡ 1-Click AI Pitch Generator:**
   - Generates customized **Cold Email Pitches** (with compelling subject lines and conversion-focused body text).
   - Generates punchy, mobile-optimized **Instagram / Facebook DMs** (ideal for businesses without websites).
   - 1-Click "Copy to Clipboard" with instant feedback.

3. **Omnichannel 1-Click Outreach:**
   - **Direct WhatsApp:** Opens a pre-composed personalized conversation with the contractor or business owner in 1 click.
   - **Direct Email / SMTP:** Send cold pitch emails automatically via configured SMTP or standard mail client.
   - **Live Pipeline Tracker:** Update stages from `New` ➔ `Pitched` ➔ `Followed Up` ➔ `Meeting Booked` ➔ `Closed ($$$)`.

4. **🤖 24/7 Autopilot Hunting:**
   - Autonomous batch hunting across 12 high-ticket niches and 15 Tier-1 wealth metro markets.
   - Smart deduplication by business phone, domain, and business name.

5. **💼 Full-Screen Master Database Workspace:**
   - Click **"Master Database"** in the top navigation to expand into a true full-screen data workspace (press `ESC` anytime to return).
   - **Segmented View Tabs**: Instantly switch between `All Leads`, `No Website`, `Has Website`, `Pitched`, and `Meeting Booked`.
   - **Clean & Minimal SaaS Grid**: High-legibility typography, clear data hierarchy, and refined badges (no cluttered AI-template aesthetics).
   - **Real-Time Filtering**: Instant live search across company names, niches, cities, and email contacts.
   - **"Copy for Sheets"** (TSV format): 1-click buffer copying to paste directly (`Ctrl + V`) into Google Sheets or Excel with clean alignment.
   - **"Export CSV"**: Instant 1-click lifetime leads export.
